Yes. Every formula is pH-balanced, paraben-free and skin-tested.

Within 24 hours in Tuguegarao City and 2 to 4 days for nearby areas.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash on delivery, GCash and bank transfer.

Can I return a product?

Message us within 3 days if an item arrives damaged and we will replace it.

Do you offer bulk orders?

Yes. Ask about dorm, office and event bundles.
